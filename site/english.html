RESEARCH

Research Themes

Making materials, observing interfaces, and understanding reactions.

01 / PHOTOELECTROCHEMISTRY

Solid-State Photoelectrochemistry and Photo-Rechargeable Batteries

I investigate photoelectrochemical reactions at semiconductor/solid-electrolyte interfaces. The goal is to create materials and devices that convert and store solar energy through light-driven lithium-ion (de)intercalation.

02 / SOLID-STATE BATTERIES

Interfaces and Microstructures in All-Solid-State Batteries

I study how composite-electrode microstructures and solid-electrolyte mechanical properties affect battery operation. Operando microscopy and interface analysis reveal conductive pathways and reaction distributions.

03 / PHOTOCATALYSIS

Water-Splitting Photocatalysts and Artificial Photosynthesis

I develop metal-oxide photocatalysts that produce hydrogen and oxygen from water using sunlight, including materials with band-structure control and photocatalyst–electrolysis hybrid systems.
